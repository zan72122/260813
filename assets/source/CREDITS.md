# Texture sources

All materials below are CC0 (public domain equivalent) from ambientCG.
Files here are downsized and re-encoded; `tools/bake-textures.mjs` turns
them into the lit tiles the game actually loads.

- **wood** — ambientCG Wood095 (CC0)
- **floor** — ambientCG WoodFloor051 (CC0)
- **bamboo** — ambientCG Bamboo002A (CC0)
- **cloth** — ambientCG Carpet016 (CC0)
- **snow** — ambientCG Snow007A (CC0)

Baked outputs live in `assets/baked/` and are what the game loads.
Regenerate with `node tools/bake-textures.mjs`.
